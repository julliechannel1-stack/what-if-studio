But here is the thing, and it is the real surprise, and it changes everything you thought this story was about. The Moon does far more than light the night and pull the tides. NASA says it plainly: the Moon stabilizes our planet's wobble, which has made the climate less variable over thousands of years. Picture Earth as a spinning top, leaning as it turns, and picture the Moon as a steady hand resting lightly on that top, keeping its lean from wandering too far. Without that hand, the top still spins, and the lean still holds for now, but nothing stops it from drifting, slowly, year after year, century after century. The night sky was only the opening scene. The true danger is not darkness. It is a planet that can no longer hold its balance, and the tilt that gives you summer and winter is starting to slip. The slow wobble it prevents is the difference between a planet with steady seasons and a planet whose climate wanders wherever chance pushes it.
