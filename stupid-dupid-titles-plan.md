## Arc — What If Your Crush Was The Last Girl On Earth? (drafted 2026-11-20, ready to script)

Slug: crush-last-girl-on-earth. Style: v3 Talk-to-Animals sample — Pip dark navy hoodie, yellow drawstrings, white face, teal shoes, orange glow; rich bedroom; TALK bubbles; no floating word text; no title card cold open. Length target: about six minutes, seven segments, about one thousand words. No digits anywhere in the script — spell every number out in words.

Hook: You wake up to a silent phone, an empty street, and a school with no one in it, and then you find her — the only other person left on Earth is your crush.

Turn: Two people cannot restart the world. Scientists say about fifty breeding adults to avoid inbreeding in the short term and about five hundred to stay healthy for generations, so the last two people on Earth are not the start of a new world, they are its ending — unless they save what the world knew.

Payoff: The thing you always wanted — her, alone, with no one else in the way — turns out to mean something bigger than a crush: learning everything, saving everything, and choosing each other on purpose.

Comment question (s07, exact text): If you were the last two people on Earth, what is the first thing you would save, and why?

### s01
You wake up to a phone with no signal and a street with no sound, and your school is empty, the shops are empty, the whole city is empty, and you walk home calling out for anyone until you turn a corner and see her standing in the middle of the road, and she is the only other person left on Earth, and she is your crush.

### s02
For one perfect day the empty world is yours, as you take whatever you want from silent shops and eat ice cream for dinner and ride a bus with no driver, and she laughs like she never laughed at school, and for the first time there is no one watching, no one judging, and no one standing between you and her.

### s03
Then the quiet starts to press on you, because a city with no voices is too big and too still, and the nights are the worst, with no traffic and no neighbours and no one else awake anywhere, and you understand that people are not background noise, they are the thing that makes a place feel alive, and without them even a perfect day feels hollow.

### s04
Here is the turn, and it changes everything you thought this story was about: two people cannot restart humanity, because scientists say about fifty breeding adults are needed to avoid inbreeding in the short term and about five hundred to keep enough variety for generations, so you and she are not the beginning of a new world, you are the last page of the old one, and the question is no longer who likes who, it is what survives.

### s05
So you stop playing and start saving, as you carry books out of the library and seeds out of the garden shop and learn to grow food and fix a generator and keep the lights on, and she keeps the lists and you keep the tools, and the two of you become a team, because knowledge is the only thing that can outlive the people who made it.

### s06
And somewhere between the planting and the fixing, the crush changes into something steadier, because liking someone from across a classroom costs nothing, but choosing the same person every morning in an empty world costs everything, and when she takes your hand it is not a school crush any more, it is a promise, and you finally understand what she was worth all along.

### s07
Here is the payoff the empty world leaves you with: you always wanted her all to yourself, and now you have her, and it turns out the real gift was never being alone with her, it was becoming the kind of person she would choose to rebuild with, and the episode ends on the comment question card asking what you would save first if you were the last two people on Earth, and why.

Sets: bedroom-rich (reuse), empty street / school (new, simple), silent supermarket (new, one set), library with seed packets (new, simple).
